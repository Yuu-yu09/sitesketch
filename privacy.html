Privacy & cookie preferences

Plain English, and short. SiteSketch is a school project, so there is no marketing team here to hide behind.

The short version

  • We set one cookie, to keep you signed in.
  • We use no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.
  • We never see or store your password — not even hashed in a way we could read.
  • Nothing you write is sold, shared or used to profile you.
  • Your published site is public to anyone who has its address. That is the point of publishing it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA you have the right to see what we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to get a portable copy, and to object to processing. Because this is a demonstration project, the practical answer is: delete your projects in the builder, and delete your account from the dashboard, and the rows go with it. If you want a copy of your data or want something removed that you cannot remove yourself, ask and it will be done by hand.

Children

SiteSketch is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their details. If you believe a child has signed up, tell us and the account will be removed.

Changes to this page

If this policy changes we will say so here, and the date below will move. Material changes will also re-open the cookie notice rather than being applied silently to people who had already decided.
